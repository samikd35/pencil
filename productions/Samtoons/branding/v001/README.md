# SamToons — YouTube channel kit

16 September 2026. User confirmed that SamToons is exclusively football comedy. The childhood-story wording was a mistake and is superseded.

## Channel direction

Original animated football comedy for fans who enjoy match banter, player caricatures, manager reactions, and exaggerated retellings of football drama. The Manchester derby film is the first production. Build each video around one recognizable football story, a comic premise, escalating reactions, and a payoff. Preserve the real event's essential actions and outcome while inventing the cartoon dialogue and exaggeration. Present films as animated parody.

- `samtoons-youtube-banner.png`: exactly 2048 × 1152. Central mascot, name, and tagline fit the phone safe area.

## Visual identity

The mascot is a bold S with expressive eyes, a raised eyebrow, and a grin. Its simple silhouette follows the film's emphasis on expressive cartoon acting. The banner adds football doodles without tying the channel to one club or fixture. The logo is coral with dark ink outlines on cream; these are intended reference colors rather than guarantees of exact generated pixels: coral #EF634E, ink #202330, cream #FFF4DC. Use generous space, thick outlines, and rounded lettering. Keep facial details legible at small sizes.

Tagline: **Football. Drama. Punchlines.**

## Validation and provenance

Reviewed two frames from the delivered derby film for visual context. Artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool, inspected, and revised for banner cropping and profile background. Final dimensions normalized with macOS sips; both PNG files are below YouTube's upload size limits. The central banner crop was inspected after resizing: the complete mascot, channel name, and tagline survive.

YouTube specifies a 1235 × 338 text/logo safe area at this banner size: [official branding guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en). The outer football doodles are decorative and may disappear in narrower device views.
