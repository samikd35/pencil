## The two controversies: precise boundaries

**Red card:** Foden's dismissal is established. Whether Bruno should also have been sent off is an argument voiced by Maresca and pundits, not a second sending-off or a verified later reversal. [Maresca's official written reaction](https://live.mancity.com/news/mens/enzo-maresca-manchester-derby-written-verdict-63924926) accepts Foden's red and argues Bruno warranted one too. Reports describe Bruno's preceding contact differently: City's report says he kicked grounded Foden; Sky describes the ball being kicked into Foden's midriff. Inspect the actual clip before drawing the precise foot/ball/body contacts. Avoid invented punches, repeated blows or a claimed injury.

**Goal:** Haaland's onside position and Fernández's effect on an opponent are two different questions. Fernández did not touch the ball; that alone did not settle interference. Pro Ref's subsequent statement says VAR missed the likely impact of his position and should have recommended an on-field review. It confirms contact with United to acknowledge the judgment error and promises a review. Sky additionally reports that United received an apology. This does **not** establish a personal apology by Michael Oliver or a filmed confrontation. A delivery of an acknowledgment letter after the game is a defensible fictional gag; an Oliver speech must not be attributed to an actual interview. An obviously invented, sobbing Oliver apologizing to Carrick is the user-authorized parody treatment. [Statement and match officials](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/33694/13585607/erling-haaland-s-man-city-goal-decision-vs-man-utd-an-error-of-judgement-says-pro-ref).
