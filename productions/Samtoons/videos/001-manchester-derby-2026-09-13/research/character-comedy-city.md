# City character comedy — research seeds and original gags

Researched 14 September 2026 for the Manchester derby film. The user permits invented dialogue, behavior and exaggerated performances, and is open to a 150–180-second film. These are proposals for character comedy, not an approved script or duration change.

The most useful combination is **Maresca trying to escape the Pep comparison** and **Donnarumma as a gentle giant who becomes a goalkeeper wolf**. Both read visually, can recur, and serve the actual match. The research supplies a recognizable starting point; the performances and dialogue below are original inventions.

## 1. Maresca: “My own manager,” everyone else's Pep lookalike

**Real seed.** In his first City press conference, Maresca explicitly rejected copying Guardiola and said he wanted to implement his own football. This is a first-party, current-season statement, not a nickname inferred from their appearance. [City's July 2026 article](https://www.mancity.com/news/mens/enzo-maresca-written-embargo-63920496). A City fan thread independently makes the visual comparison: user NovellaJokes says the sight of Maresca drinking water briefly looked like Pep. That proves an attributable fan joke exists; it does not measure its popularity across football. [Direct r/MCFC thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/MCFC/comments/1vwa1kr/he_really_does_look_like_pep/).

**Additional real behavior.** Levi Colwill described Maresca's football as a chess game, with every piece needing its correct place, and emphasized its mental demands. This gives us a coach-specific prop beyond baldness. [Chelsea's first-party Colwill interview](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/levi-colwill-interview-the-joy-of-maresca-and-becoming-a-senior-player).

**Original running performance.** Maresca is proud, painstaking and increasingly offended by mistaken identity. Give him his own facial construction, beard and silhouette; resemblance should not erase who he is.

- **Entrance:** A stadium attendant cheerfully says, “Welcome back, Pep!” Maresca thrusts an oversized ENZO badge towards the camera. “Different man. Different ideas.”
- **Escalation:** His tactics board is a tiny chessboard. After Foden's red, a red-card hand plucks one blue piece away. Maresca's eye twitches; he rearranges the remaining pieces with theatrical precision and slides a goalkeeper-shaped wolf into place.
- **Payoff:** After the win, an attendant offers him a “Pep of the Match” plaque. Maresca snaps it in half, reveals ENZO written on his shirt underneath, then quietly pockets the trophy anyway.

**Use:** Strong recurring identity, with 2–3 short appearances. The chess rearrangement should happen after the dismissal; the joke need not pretend the red was his plan. “Diet Pep” appears in secondary coverage of a 2024 press conference, but the primary statement plus current fan reaction are enough; avoid building a scene around an unviewed clip.

## 2. Donnarumma: the gentle giant who guards the goal like a wolf

**Real seed.** Haaland called Donnarumma a “wolf in the goal” after this derby and praised the late save. [City's official match centre, Haaland post-match section](https://www.mancity.com/matchday/fixtures/mens/2026/premier-league/man-united-v-man-city-13-sept-2026). UEFA has described him as a gentle giant, and City's own magazine documents his imposing 6ft 5in stature. [UEFA profile](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/026b-12bc726f4d2d-ac537c0175b4-1000--donnarumma-completes-italy-s-renaissance/), [City November 2025 magazine](https://live.mancity.com/features/citymag/nov2025/). “Gigio” is an established familiar name used by his clubs; the wolf is an attributable derby description, not a proven lifelong nickname.

**Original running performance.** Warm, enormous and almost sleepy between attacks; instantly predatory when the ball threatens the goal.

- **Plant:** A friendly wave from Gigio's giant glove briefly covers the entire goal. He politely lifts it so the referee can finish inspecting the net.
- **Escalation:** After one of the real post strikes, he pats the upright like a fellow guard. A tiny wolf ear flicks up when another attack approaches, then disappears.
- **Climax:** Mbeumo sees daylight and shoots. Gigio's shadow becomes a huge wolf; the actual saving hand stretches into a cartoon paw. He makes the save, shrinks back to normal, and calmly straightens his gloves.

**Use:** Best physical-action character for the finish. Keep Mbeumo's attempt and Donnarumma's save readable before the transformation. The wolf, paw and post friendship are freely invented animation, not claims about match footage.

## 3. Donnarumma: delayed celebration and extreme composure

**Real seed.** Donnarumma's subdued immediate reaction after the winning EURO 2020 penalty save became a recognizable moment. In a later UEFA interview he explained that he looked at the referee because VAR checks might still be needed, then saw everyone running around and understood what they had achieved. [Donnarumma's UEFA interview, 4 October 2021](https://www.uefa.com/news/026e-13660424e964-a2457f6396e0-1000--donnarumma-on-italy-vs-spain/). The 2021 incident is a historical character reference, not an event from this derby.

**Original joke.** At the final whistle, everybody piles onto him while he remains a straight-faced goalkeeper statue. He looks at the referee, the scoreboard, and his gloves. “Finished?” The rest of the team has already left before he produces one very small celebratory fist pump.

**Use:** A 2–4-second reaction tag attached to the wolf climax. It is funnier as a change of scale after a huge save than as an extended flashback explaining the 2021 final. Do not present lack of intelligence as the factual explanation for the real reaction.

## 4. Enzo Fernández: the enthusiastic returning student / the wrong Enzo

**Real seed.** Fernández's deadline-day arrival reunited him with his former Chelsea manager. In City's own interview he said he had learned much from Maresca, described him as passionate, and credited his effort in bringing him to the club. [City's first-party Fernández interview, September 2026](https://www.mancity.com/news/mens/enzo-fernandez-on-enzo-maresca-63923952). Their shared first name is immediately legible; an established universal “teacher's pet” meme was **not** verified.

**Original joke.** A reporter calls, “Enzo!” Both turn, both point at themselves, then both answer at once. Maresca pulls out his ENZO badge from the Pep joke; Fernández proudly shows an identical one. For the goal controversy, Fernández puts on a ridiculous transparent raincoat labelled “NOT INVOLVED” while standing directly in everyone's way. His manager proudly gives a classroom thumbs-up.

**Use:** Supporting gag, not a second plot. The teacher/student relationship and invisible raincoat are invented. The invisible-man behavior exaggerates the disputed offside involvement; it does not require Enzo to have used those words or a specific pre-existing nickname. His real Chelsea history establishes familiarity with Maresca, not a claim that selection is personal favoritism.

## 5. City: the self-appointed kings of Manchester

**Real seed.** Elliot Anderson called City the “Kings of Manchester” in his first club interview. [Official City interview](https://www.mancity.com/citytv/mens/elliot-anderson-says-he-has-joined-the-kings-of-manchester-watch-his-first-manchester-city-interview-63920407). The [official derby match centre](https://www.mancity.com/matchday/fixtures/mens/2026/premier-league/man-united-v-man-city-13-sept-2026) records a post-match answer reaffirming that boast. This concerns local bragging rights, not winning the 2026/27 league in September.

**Original visual motif.** City arrive carrying an absurdly large crown. Foden's dismissal makes it topple; Haaland props it up when he scores; at full time the crown is too small for Giant Gigio and sits between his wolf ears. Anderson, who made the boast, solemnly presents it like a state occasion while everyone else celebrates messily.

**Use:** A flexible opening/payoff prop if the film needs a shared visual thread. It is clearer and more relevant to this match than a financial-charges joke, and it makes the rivalry visible without extra exposition. No researched misconduct allegation is needed.

## Best integration

1. **Maresca/Pep mistaken identity:** entrance, tactics reaction after Foden's red, short callback after the win. Character engine: determination to be recognized as himself.
2. **Gentle Giant Gigio → wolf:** one early visual plant, late Mbeumo save transformation, tiny deadpan celebration. Character engine: enormous defensive power with an unexpectedly calm personality.

Enzo's two-name confusion can connect to Maresca with one quick beat. The crown is optional. Five researched seeds do not require five full running jokes; let two characters carry the comic continuity while the match supplies the escalation.
