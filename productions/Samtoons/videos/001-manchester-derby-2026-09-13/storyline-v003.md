# Ten Men. Two Posts. No Points.

**Samtoons · Manchester United 0–1 Manchester City · 13 September 2026**  
**Storyline v003 · 2:30 working length · Bruno overreaction revision**

The story follows United's hope repeatedly rising and collapsing. After Foden's red card, Bruno thinks the hard part is over. City's goalposts disagree. Then VAR, Haaland and Donnarumma take turns spoiling the afternoon. Michael Oliver eventually arrives to apologize so dramatically that Carrick needs an umbrella—and Foden finally gets a fishing spot. United still get no points.

This is comedy built around the match. All dialogue below and the impossible performances are original parody, including Oliver's apology. The sources establish the sporting events and give us joke material; they do not claim these cartoon conversations occurred. Landscape 16:9 and short English dialogue are proposed.

## Character personalities that drive the jokes

- **Bruno:** the walking complaints department. A complaint scroll first gets him the result he wants, then becomes useless as the afternoon goes wrong.
- **Foden:** the fisherman who has been baited. His exit with a fishing rod sets up the final flood gag.
- **Haaland:** the happy scoring robot. His celebration buffers during VAR; his manager-recognition software still thinks Maresca is Pep.
- **Maresca:** enjoying the wins, increasingly irritated that everyone sees Pep. His name badge and full-time correction make the resemblance a character conflict.
- **Donnarumma:** a friendly giant until the ball threatens his goal; then a wolf. He returns to calm immediately after the huge save.
- **Rashford and Maguire:** brief, specific performances using the temple-point celebration and the oversized aerial-threat persona.
- **Carrick and Old Trafford:** the exhausted straight man and a stadium capable of joining him in tears.

These are selected comic characterizations, not factual personality diagnoses. The [United](research/character-comedy-united.md), [City](research/character-comedy-city.md), and [Haaland/Foden](research/character-comedy-haaland-foden.md) research notes give dated sources and separate known traits or fan jokes from our inventions.
