## Final generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: finished portrait thumbnail / cover artwork for the SamToons football comedy Short, 9:16 composition, preferably 1080 x 1920 pixels.
Primary request: make an instantly readable, funny, bold thumbnail for a cartoon in which Manchester United hit the post twice and the talking goalpost demands a new contract.
Input images: Image 1 is a character and art-style reference for the actual talking white goalpost with two black oval eyes, small cream pupils, simple dark mouth and sky-blue number 1. Image 2 is a character and art-style reference for Bruno Fernandes with black swept hair, black beard, red football shirt, white shoulder stripes and captain armband. Preserve their identities and the simple thick-ink cartoon aesthetic, but recompose into a dramatically closer, expressive thumbnail.
Composition: a very large expressive white goalpost on the left, proudly smirking with its sky-blue numeral 1 visible below its face; an oversized recognizable Bruno head and upper torso on the right, mouth open in shocked disbelief, palms up. Both faces dominate the central area and look toward each other. The goalpost must remain recognizably a straight white goal upright connected to a crossbar, with faint goal net behind; NOT a person, not a football-shaped head. Keep characters distinct with thick dark outlines. Add a single small football near the lower portion and a few simple impact marks to imply the post's saves. Keep the drawing as clear as the provided film, with slightly more polished flat cel shading, no photorealism.
Background: simple dark ink navy stadium and subtle goal net with a warm coral comic burst behind the characters, minimal visual noise. Sky blue accents on the post, vivid red on Bruno, warm cream typography.
Text verbatim: "PAY ME!" in enormous bold condensed uppercase comic lettering inside one warm cream speech bubble with a short tail unambiguously pointing at the goalpost. Place this above the faces, inside the central safe composition with generous outer margins. Only other text is a small tasteful "SamToons" wordmark near the bottom. The numeral "1" on the post and "C" on the armband are allowed.
Keep main text and both faces within the middle 75% of the canvas height so they stay readable when cropped. Strong mobile-size readability. A real finished cover, no mockup, no surrounding device, no multi-panel layout.
Avoid: extra captions, tiny text, scoreboards, fake results, invented trophies, money, contracts, new characters, emoji, arrows, unnecessary clutter, glossy 3D, photorealistic faces, copying the screenshot subtitles.

## Export and review

Generated at 941 × 1672, normalized to 1080 × 1920 and JPEG with macOS sips. Reviewed the output for the two characters, the goalpost/football premise, complete “PAY ME!” lettering, SamToons spelling and portrait composition.

## Upload

YouTube's current help page says custom Shorts thumbnails can be uploaded in YouTube Studio on a computer with a verified account. Open Content → Shorts → select the Short → Thumbnail → Upload file → Save.

Source, checked 2026-09-16: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en-GB

The July announcement described an initial YouTube Partner Program rollout; account availability may vary. If Upload file is absent, the YouTube mobile app still offers selection of an existing video frame. This generated cover is not embedded in the MP4.
