## Ferguson's role

He has the confidence of somebody explaining the game to people who ought to know better. Start with clipped, controlled contempt; let disbelief and irritation break through at specific reversals. Continuous shouting would remove the contrast.

Whenever we cut to him before the ending, show a backlit silhouette with no readable face. Use brief punctuation shots rather than covering the football with a narrator inset. No nameplate, revealing portrait or reflection before the reveal. Save conspicuous identity cues for the final lit view. The joke should still land for viewers who guess the identity early.

His rivalry narration can be completely unfair. Arteta's absurd tactics may genuinely work inside our invented cartoon; no balancing correction is required.

## Player arrangement under discussion

Proposed interpretation of “9–1”: **nine outfield players blocking the goal, Raya in front of them, Gyökeres alone upfield**. All eleven can be established across the wide coverage; no player silently disappears. The user may instead prefer all eleven physically crammed around the goal. Preserve that choice if selected and rewrite the final joke accordingly.

The nine-player arrangement should read as bodies with distinct poses, not nine overlapping heads. Exact drawing views and supporting-player identities follow after the writing choice; a frontal lineup alone does not provide the turns and running views.

## How transitions should work through the film

Use the user's principle: **the end of one scene creates the reason for the next.** Here, the corner produces the lead; the lead triggers Arteta's command; the command produces the defensive pile-up; the stranded striker gives us the callback. We do not need an announcer saying “Next, their defence.”

A possible onward link, only if the City chase is the next chosen chapter: Arteta says “Nobody is getting past us,” then a City-results notification interrupts him. This is an optional connector, not a requirement to place the chase immediately afterward. Choose the surrounding chapters before locking that cut.

## A possible final reveal

After the title outcome, return to the same narrator silhouette. A lamp clicks on. For the first time we see Ferguson's face and the United context around him. He slowly folds his notes.

**Ferguson, utterly serious:** “I retired. I didn't say I'd moved on.”

Hold on his expression rather than immediately adding another gag. This is an original proposed closing line, not a quotation. The reveal identity is selected; this reveal action and punchline are not yet selected.
