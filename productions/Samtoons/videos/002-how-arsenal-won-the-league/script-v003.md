## 1. The delivery — 0:00–0:30

**N01.** A seated figure watches football in a dark room. We see only his silhouette from behind. A remote clicks. Two quiet musical notes suggest a third that never arrives.

A short camera push ends as he leans back, completely comfortable.

**NARRATOR — dry, certain:** “Relax. It’s Arsenal. We all know how this ends.”

**OP01.** Cut to Arsenal’s preseason training ground. A rival supporter wheels in a crate marked **RUNNERS-UP**. His trolley has one persistently squeaky wheel.

**NARRATOR:** “Before a ball was kicked, their runners-up medals had arrived.”

The courier opens the crate and offers Arteta a silver medal.

**COURIER — cheerful:** “Same order as usual?”

**OP02.** Arteta looks at the medal, then the courier.

**ARTETA:** “We haven’t played a game.”

**COURIER:** “Exactly. Beat the rush.”

Hold Arteta’s offended stare for a beat. The courier keeps smiling.

**OP03.** Arteta shuts the crate and takes out his phone.

**ARTETA:** “Get me a striker.”

The phone-button sound leads into applause.

## 2. The actual star signing — 0:30–1:00

**TR01.** Match Arteta putting down his phone to Gyökeres putting down his bag at an unveiling. A red carpet awaits. Gyökeres straightens up and steps toward it.

**NARRATOR:** “So they bought a striker. Someone to finish the chances.”

**TR02.** Arteta proudly gestures along the carpet. The camera follows it past Gyökeres to a **corner flag wearing a number-nine shirt**.

The applause swells for the flag. Cut back to Gyökeres; the applause stops.

**GYÖKERES:** “Then why am I here?”

**ARTETA — entirely sincere:** “Win us a corner.”

**TR03.** Cut to training. Gyökeres is through on goal. He draws back his shooting foot.

Arteta frantically points at a practice defender.

**ARTETA:** “Not the goal! Off him!”

Gyökeres reluctantly kicks the ball off the defender and over the byline. Arteta applauds like he has just witnessed a masterpiece.

**ARTETA:** “Now we’re dangerous.”

**TR04.** The practice corner flag snaps upright. Match cut to the same flag at a crowded match. Training ambience becomes a stadium roar. Saka approaches the ball.

## 3. That’s enough football — 1:00–1:40

**CD01.** Saka delivers a corner. Gabriel rises among the opposing defenders.

**NARRATOR:** “Their attacking philosophy? Win a corner. Somebody’s forehead will do the rest.”

Header. Net. **1–0.** Finish the line before the crowd’s goal reaction.

**CD02.** Gabriel starts celebrating. Arteta sees the score and immediately beckons everyone toward Arsenal’s goal.

**ARTETA — cutting through the celebration:** “ONE–NIL! That’s enough football. Everybody back!”

**CD03.** Follow Gabriel’s turn into the retreat. Nine outfield players squeeze shoulder-to-shoulder across the goalmouth, shuffling to fit. One has to tuck his elbows in.

Raya proudly steps in front of them. Footsteps and boot squeaks stop together; one gate-clunk accent completes the absurd barrier. Hold the whole arrangement briefly.

**CD04.** An opposing attacker looks at Raya, then at the wall of Arsenal shirts behind him. Raya waves him away.

**NARRATOR:** “Lovely. A goalkeeper protecting nine defenders.”

**CD05.** Cut very wide. Gyökeres is alone upfield, waving toward his distant teammates.

**GYÖKERES — calling across the pitch:** “What am I supposed to do?”

**ARTETA:** “Win another corner!”

Gyökeres slowly lowers his arm.

**N02.** A brief cutaway: the narrator’s silhouetted hand crushes his prepared notes. His two-note motif returns, ending on the paper crunch. No dialogue.

Back to Arteta admiring his barrier.

**ARTETA:** “Nobody is getting past us.”

His phone pings: **CITY WIN**. His smile vanishes. Let the ping interrupt his satisfied pose before he answers.

**ARTETA — suddenly quiet:** “Again?”

The nine-player barrier belongs only to this scene. All eleven are accounted for: nine outfield players in the goalmouth, Raya in front and Gyökeres upfield.

## 4. Why is he walking? — 1:40–2:30

**CP01.** Push into the league table on Arteta’s phone; its first-place row becomes a road through winter and spring toward May. Seasonal roadside markers establish time passing without stopping for chapter cards. Arsenal carry an awkward podium marked **1st**. Gabriel, Saliba and Gyökeres bear its weight; Arteta directs them while other players run alongside.

**NARRATOR:** “They could defend a one–nil. They couldn’t defend a lead on the table.”

Their hurried footsteps replace the stadium sound. Pep follows behind, hands in his pockets, walking.

**CP02.** Arteta glances back. Pep is a long way away.

**ARTETA — briefly reassured:** “He’s only walking. We’re fine.”

Another **CITY WIN** ping. Repeat the same backward view: Pep is much closer. His walking pace has not changed.

Arteta accelerates. The carriers stumble as they try to keep up.

**CP03.** Cut wide enough to compare Arsenal’s full sprint with Pep’s ordinary stroll.

**ARTETA — gasping:** “WHY IS HE WALKING?”

**PEP — without breaking his stride:** “Long season, Mikel.”

Let Arteta process the reply before he panics harder.

**CP04.** Arteta points ahead.

**ARTETA:** “Eyes forward! Nobody look back!”

Widen farther. The rival’s squeaky medal trolley is keeping pace on their other side. He checks his clipboard.

**COURIER:** “Still delivering in May?”

**ARTETA:** “NOT NOW!”

**CP05.** Arteta looks back anyway. The podium’s front corner slips; Gabriel lunges and grabs it.

**ARTETA:** “Keep hold of the lead!”

Match Gabriel’s gripping hand to him grabbing an opponent’s shirt in a match. Immediately widen to establish the penalty area. The road metaphor ends here.

## 5. Your call is very important — 2:30–3:20

**VH01.** Gabriel’s shirt pull sends the attacker down. The referee sees it, blows his whistle and points to the spot.

**REFEREE — confident:** “Penalty. Against Arsenal.”

He presses his headset.

**VH02.** A cheerful phone menu appears beside him.

**RECORDED VOICE:** “For a penalty against Arsenal, please hold.”

Thin, cheerful hold music. His patient expression begins to fade.

**VH03.** Cut to the fictional VAR office. A grey handset flashes beside a **PENDING** tray. The operator ignores it.

A gold phone lights up: **MIKEL**. Two bright rings. The operator sits upright and answers instantly, suddenly all warmth.

**OPERATOR:** “Mikel! Straight through, as always.”

**VH04.** Arteta discreetly shields his phone at the touchline.

**ARTETA — relaxed:** “Can you make that go away?”

**OPERATOR:** “Of course. Would you like a corner with that?”

**ARTETA:** “Make it two.”

The operator presses a large **PLAY ON** button and slides the referee’s handset deeper into the pending tray. A neat confirmation ding accompanies the button.

**VH05.** A stadium signal says **PLAY ON**. Football resumes around the referee. The fouled attacker protests; the referee holds up one finger for “wait,” still listening.

**REFEREE:** “Hello? I’m still here.”

**RECORDED VOICE:** “Your call is very important to us.”

The hold music resumes. His expression says he no longer believes this.

Two brief time-passing cuts: same waiting pose, changed match board and departing spectators. A snack wrapper appears beside him. Keep the bridge short. The hold tune fades into the crowd at the title-deciding match.

## 6. Exactly as rehearsed — 3:20–4:15

**FR01.** Establish the corner, the crowded box and a level score. Saka places the ball. Gabriel and Gyökeres wait among the defenders; the opposing goalkeeper watches them. The courier stands beside Arteta, outside the playing area, with his medal crate already open.

**NARRATOR — struggling to contain his irritation:** “One goal for the title. And of course, one bloody corner.”

**FR02.** Gyökeres gestures toward himself from the box.

**GYÖKERES:** “Do I finally get to shoot?”

**ARTETA:** “No. Stand there.”

Gyökeres stays put but turns toward Arteta, palms up in frustration.

**N03.** The narrator pitches forward in his chair. We see only his shadow. The camera begins a small move toward his side but cuts away before exposing his face.

**NARRATOR — clinging to hope:** “There’s still time.”

Chair creak. The two-note motif accelerates, then stops as Saka starts his run-up.

**FR03.** Saka delivers. Gabriel jumps under pressure. The ball sails beyond him.

Gyökeres is still addressing Arteta.

**GYÖKERES:** “I’m supposed to be the stri—”

**BONK.** The ball hits his fully clothed backside and changes direction into the goal, wrong-footing the keeper.

Show the delivery, missed header, accidental contact and goal in readable coverage. Keep the decisive contact visible; no extra ricochets or replay needed. Briefly drop the crowd on the bonk, then bring the roar back as the ball crosses the line and the net moves.

**FR04.** Gyökeres turns, sees the goal and points at himself.

**GYÖKERES:** “That counts?”

Arteta folds his arms.

**ARTETA — utterly sincere:** “Exactly as rehearsed.”

A final whistle confirms the result. The table changes to **1 ARSENAL / 2 CITY**.

**FR05.** The courier looks at his unused silver medals.

**COURIER:** “So… who signs for these?”

Arteta points toward City’s second-place entry.

**ARTETA:** “Wrong address.”

The courier closes the crate. A medal clinks; the trolley squeaks away.

**VH06.** Cut briefly to the exhausted referee. His hold music finally stops.

**REFEREE — suddenly hopeful:** “Finally!”

**RECORDED VOICE:** “Thank you for waiting. The season has now ended.”

Disconnect. Hold his face for a beat. Celebration sound carries us into the trophy lift.

## 7. The narrator loses it — 4:15–4:40

**N04a.** Arsenal lift the trophy. Include Gyökeres with his celebrating teammates.

Return to the narrator’s room. Stadium sound narrows into distant television-speaker sound. He sits motionless. The camera reaches a frontal view; his face remains dark.

The familiar two musical notes return slowly.

**N04b.** He switches on the desk lamp. On the click, the missing third note finally lands.

**Sir Alex Ferguson.**

Hold his face for about one second so recognition lands. End the music. An audible breath fills the quiet.

**N04c.** He shoots out of his chair. Cut wider on the movement to show his body, desk and television.

**FERGUSON — exploding at the television:** “I SAID I WAS RETIRED!”

A ragged breath. His outrage becomes a helpless appeal to the ceiling.

**FERGUSON:** “STOP GIVING ME REASONS TO COME BACK!”

**N04d.** He grabs the notes he crushed earlier and hurls them at the screen.

A tiny paper **tap**. The notes fall. Arsenal keep celebrating on television.

Hold his furious, powerless stare. Cut to black. No further tag.
