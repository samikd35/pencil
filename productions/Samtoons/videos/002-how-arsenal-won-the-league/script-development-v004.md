## Ferguson's role

He has the confidence of somebody explaining the game to people who ought to know better. Start with clipped, controlled contempt; let disbelief and irritation break through at specific reversals. Continuous shouting would remove the contrast.

Whenever we cut to him before the ending, show a backlit silhouette with no readable face. Use brief punctuation shots rather than covering the football with a narrator inset. No nameplate, revealing portrait or reflection before the reveal. Save conspicuous identity cues for the final lit view. The joke should still land for viewers who guess the identity early.

His rivalry narration can be completely unfair. Arteta's absurd tactics may genuinely work inside our invented cartoon; no balancing correction is required.

### Sound, camera and concealment rules for this proposal

- **Music:** one small, slightly pompous suspense motif. Change its rhythm as his composure slips; reserve its final note for the face reveal. Keep it out of normal football sketches so it belongs specifically to the concealed narrator. No continuous thriller score or ominous trailer booms. The sound is an original planning intention; no instrument, track or recording is selected or auditioned yet.
- **Sound effects and silence:** remote click → paper crush → chair creak → lamp click. Each belongs to a visible action and becomes clearer as match noise recedes. The biggest reveal accent is the contrast with quiet, not a louder explosion.
- **Camera and editing:** wide confidence → detail of tension → tighter reactive silhouette → frontal reveal. A push or turn must discover an action or new information. Hold still for facial recognition, then cut wider on his explosive rise so the body action stays readable. Avoid constant drifting and repeated fake reveals.
- **Lighting and clues:** before the ending, his body remains a shadow. No illuminated eyes, hands, reflections, recognizable desk portraits or readable United memorabilia. Conceal distinctive identity cues until the final view. Some viewers may recognize the performed voice; the reveal must reward that guess rather than depend on fooling everyone.
- **Acting:** relaxed certainty becomes contained irritation, then involuntary panic. The face reveal allows us to see the expression we have been denied all film. Do not make every narrator line a shout.

## How transitions should work through the film

Use the user's principle: **the end of one scene creates the reason for the next.** Here, the corner produces the lead; the lead triggers Arteta's command; the command produces the defensive pile-up; the stranded striker gives us the callback. We do not need an announcer saying “Next, their defence.”

His goalmouth barrier cannot stop another team collecting points. This motivates the contained title-chase sketch without keeping the exaggerated defensive formation beyond its selected scene.
