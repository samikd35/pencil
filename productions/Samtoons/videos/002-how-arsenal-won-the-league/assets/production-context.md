Profile v003 · adopted 17 September 2026. Samtoons is the user's continuing **football-comedy** production, established on 14 September and confirmed as exclusively football comedy in the [channel kit](branding/v001/README.md). The recurring format is authorized; shared plot continuity and continuing fictional arcs are not selected. Each video should work independently.

This profile supplies defaults for new Samtoons work. Each existing video retains its adopted context, assets, approvals and explicit exceptions. The [shared pipeline](../../pipeline/SKILL.md) owns production/review; Samtoons owns the creative direction below. Other niches and other football productions do not inherit it automatically.

## Audience and creative promise

Original animated football comedy for fans: a recognizable football story, character-driven jokes, escalating reactions and a clear payoff. Entertainment leads; research establishes the underlying event. Invent exaggerated dialogue, impossible visual gags and performances freely while preserving the event's essential actions and outcome. Present the work as animated parody in the appropriate publication context. Keep internal labels such as “comic fantasy” out of the picture unless they help the audience or the user requests them.

Research player, coach and club personas alongside the incident. Choose useful habits, gestures and fan memes that can support original jokes; do not add every researched joke or repeat a previous episode's ending automatically. The referee apology, VAR-door reveal and fishing payoff belong to episode 001, not a required formula.

## Visual and sound direction

Use recognizable cartoon faces, hair and kits with simple expressive bodies, coherent anatomy, readable poses and intentional timing. The accepted derby treatment is a local reference, not permission to copy body weights blindly. Plan required character views before changing camera angle. Characters, environments and surface finish are separate creative decisions; consult the general style menu only when a new treatment is unresolved.

Direct speech for its moment: outrage, pleading, dry sarcasm or restrained frustration. Accent/timbre distinguishes a character but does not supply acting. Nationality-related accents are preferences only where suitable voices exist; use distinct comic performances rather than forcing an unsupported accent. Preserve selected takes. Music is optional, must leave dialogue clear and, under the current user preference, be free to use for the intended publication with required attribution recorded separately. Do not burn music credits into video frames; provide them with publication copy.

Duration, aspect ratio and pacing are selected per video. Episode 001's 2:30–3:00 direction, landscape format and later end card are not mandatory defaults. No publishing schedule or production-time promise is set. The reusable channel identity is documented in the [branding kit](branding/v001/README.md).

## Football-specific research and staging

Choose the actual event before developing its treatment. Verify participants, result, important actions, event-specific kits, spatial relationships and what relevant footage shows. Separate verified incidents, disputed reports and invented jokes; do not present a fan theory or parody accusation as an established fact. Cite sources in the video research, and distinguish historical appearance from any deliberately selected cartoon simplification.

For match action, map the necessary attackers, defenders, keeper, ball and offscreen roles. A corner needs plausible supporting participants as well as taker and receiver, not a mechanically complete 22-player lineup. Do not invent exact historical positions; record representative simplification. Check team identity, screen direction, ball trajectory and readable contact at viewing size. Inspect kicks as coordinated support-foot, pelvis, knee, ankle, boot and ball action; a matching point in one frame is not sufficient.

These domain checks supplement the common workflow. Apply [character construction](../../pipeline/styles/construction.md#character-facing-faces-and-anatomy) for character shots and [sound direction](../../pipeline/sound-planning-method.md) when sound is used. Tool choice follows the actual shot; Blender/FFmpeg and the available voice runtime are current implementations, not the definition of football comedy.
