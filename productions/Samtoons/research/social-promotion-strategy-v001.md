# SamToons: first-video promotion strategy

16 September 2026. Prepared with the `social` skill. Working assumptions: original English-language football comedy, the finished Manchester derby film as the first release, and an initial organic promotion test with no advertising spend. “Sun Tunes” in the latest message is treated as the existing SamToons channel, not an instruction to rename it.

## Objective and audience

Attract football fans who enjoy player caricatures, match banter, and animated parody. Start with viewers interested in United, City, Haaland, and Premier League comedy. The desired outcome is more people watching and enjoying the full film, with subscribers and repeat viewers developing over subsequent releases.

The recommended campaign is **one full film → three standalone comedy clips → Shorts, Reels, and TikTok → a clear route to the full film**. Three clips is a manageable first test, not a platform requirement or promised growth formula.

## 1. Prepare the full YouTube video

Long-form videos can be discovered through Home, Suggested, and Search; external promotion is only one route. YouTube describes selection in terms of relevance and viewer response. [YouTube discovery guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en).

- Use a title that names the matchup and clearly signals comedy. Starting example: **Man United vs Man City: Even the Goalposts Joined In! | Animated Parody**.
- Use a thumbnail with one expressive character reaction and one clear comic obstacle, such as Bruno versus the talking post. Optional short text: **THE POST WINS**. Check that it reads on a phone.
- Open with the story or a joke promptly. The existing film already begins with a character interaction; preserve its timing when reviewing the opening.
- Identify the film as animated parody in its description. Keep the selected S logo and SamToons name consistent across profiles. Put the latest full film where profile visitors can easily find it.
- If the film is already public, update its presentation where necessary instead of re-uploading solely for this campaign.

## 2. Prioritize platforms

| Priority | Platform | What to publish | Route to the full film |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | YouTube Shorts, on the same channel | Three self-contained jokes from the film | Set the full film as each Short's Related Video. End with a brief invitation to tap the linked video. |
| 2 | Instagram Reels | Upload the same clean vertical clips with captions written for Instagram | Put the full-film link in the profile and share a Story with a link sticker when available. |
| 3 | TikTok | Upload the same clips and test a concise on-screen setup | Use the YouTube/profile link if available. Otherwise show the exact channel name and invite viewers to search for it. |

This ranking is a practical recommendation: Shorts offers an in-platform clickable route; Instagram and TikTok require additional steps to reach YouTube. It is not a claim that Shorts will produce a particular conversion rate.

YouTube's Related Video feature requires advanced-feature access. A plain URL in a Shorts description or comment is not clickable. [Related Video instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en), [YouTube link rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en).

Instagram supports Story link stickers, but the account must actually have the feature; Meta's announcement notes restrictions for newly created accounts. [Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/ja/news/2021/10/linkaccessstickers/). TikTok says social-account linking varies by region; its website field requires 1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account. Check the actual account before writing “link in bio.” [TikTok profile-link guidance](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account?lang=hi).

## 3. Make three clips people can enjoy on their own

These are candidate ideas grounded in the project's storyline, not a new frame-accurate review of the final film. Verify final timing before editing:

1. **The goalpost wants a contract.** Candidate opening text: “United's toughest opponent was the goalpost.” Preserve the shot, impact, reaction, and contract joke.
2. **Haaland freezes during VAR.** Candidate text: “Haaland.exe stopped responding.” Preserve enough context to understand the freeze and resumed celebration.
3. **Haaland calls Maresca ‘Pep 2.0’.** Candidate text: “When your striker still misses his old manager.” Keep the name correction and final deadpan reaction.

Aim for roughly 15–35 seconds as an initial editing brief; let the complete joke determine the actual length. Reframe for 9:16 without cutting off faces, the ball, gestures, or the payoff. Add readable speech captions, retain the original comic audio, and upload from a clean master without another platform's watermark.

Each clip should deliver its own punchline. Follow it with one short invitation: **“More derby chaos in the full cartoon.”** On Shorts, direct the viewer to the linked video; on other platforms, name the available route. Avoid withholding the entire payoff just to force a click.

## 4. Run a two-week test

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Publish or prepare the full film, title, thumbnail, profile branding, and working links. |
| Day 1 | Publish clip 1 on Shorts, Reels, and TikTok. Add an Instagram Story link if available. |
| Day 3 | Publish clip 2 on the three platforms. |
| Day 5 | Publish clip 3 on the three platforms. |
| Day 7 | Compare early performance at similar post ages and check whether the full film gained viewers. |
| Days 8–14 | Test one additional clip or a meaningfully revised opening based on evidence; begin the next full cartoon. Review again on day 14. |

These dates are a proposed experiment, not a proven best schedule. Use a manageable consistent posting window initially, then learn from actual audience data. Do not assign universal posting times before knowing the audience's timezone.

Spend about ten minutes after posting responding to viewers. Join relevant football discussions with original observations. An optional extra is sharing one appropriate clip in a fan community that welcomes self-promotion; do not scatter promotional links across unrelated comments. Outreach and publishing remain user actions unless separately requested.

## 5. Measure the outcome that matters

In YouTube Studio, review the full film's views, watch time/retention, subscribers, and reported traffic sources. For Shorts, review whether people stay to watch, retention, shares, and subscribers. Instagram/TikTok results can add reach, shares, profile visits, and link activity where available. Compare within each platform and at similar post ages; raw view counts across platforms are not a reliable like-for-like comparison. [YouTube Analytics guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en).

| Observation | Next experiment |
|---|---|
| Viewers leave the short immediately | Clarify the first visual/setup and test a faster opening. |
| Clips get attention but the full film gains little traffic | Check the route works, strengthen the reason to watch more, and improve the full film's title/thumbnail. |
| People click the full film but leave early | Check whether its opening delivers the promise of the clip and thumbnail. |
| One character or joke repeatedly earns shares and sustained viewing | Explore another original joke with that character in the next film. |

Treat these as hypotheses, especially with a small new-channel sample. Available analytics will not identify every cross-platform journey. YouTube explicitly notes that viewers do not necessarily move between short and long formats, so clip views alone cannot establish promotion success. [YouTube's cross-format guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559651?hl=en).

## First-month scope

Prioritize YouTube and reuse the three clips on Instagram and TikTok. Start without paid boosts. Reconsider paid promotion only after the material shows audience interest and there is a clear budget and measurable purpose. Keep making good full films at a sustainable pace so people who discover SamToons have a reason to return.

This document is a strategy. It does not claim the channel has been audited, the clips rendered, the accounts connected, or any content published.
