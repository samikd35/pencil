## Historical event behind the parody

The event was **Real Madrid 0–4 FC Barcelona**, La Liga matchday 11, at the Santiago Bernabéu on **26 October 2024**. It was 0–0 at half-time. Lewandowski scored in the 54th and 56th minutes, Lamine Yamal in the 77th, and Raphinha in the 84th. The animation's publication date is the following calendar day. [FC Barcelona match record](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/matches/116314/real-madrid-fc-barcelona-la-liga-2024-2025), [dated player archive](https://players.fcbarcelona.com/en/match/f2445113-real-madrid-barcelona).

The club's contemporary report confirms repeated offside frustration and two disallowed Mbappé goals; Lewandowski's first goal followed a Casadó pass and his second a Balde cross and header. It also records two missed Lewandowski chances, Yamal's finish into the upper corner, and Raphinha's chipped finish. These provide factual anchors against which the cartoon's exaggerations can be checked. FC Barcelona is a participating club and its celebratory opinions should not be treated as neutral evaluation. [26 October 2024 match report](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/4156771/real-madrid-0-4-fc-barcelona-viva-la-vida).

## First-person creator evidence

In a **9 May 2018** interview published by Reallusion, Stobbart describes combining football with animated comedy and testing songs, film parodies and recreations while finding the channel's identity. He identifies performing, voice work and comedy influences as part of his background. This is useful evidence that writing and vocal performance are integral to the format. [Creator interview](https://magazine.reallusion.com/2018/05/09/million-subscriber-youtuber-uses-crazytalk-for-sports-comedy-web-series/672375890/).

He reports a two-minute project taking roughly eight hours when existing characters and props could be reused, compared with roughly forty hours for a more ambitious four-minute parody needing new scenes, assets, effects and many voices. These are **historical self-reported examples**, not a budget or turnaround promise for Samtoons. In that interview he names CrazyTalk Animator for animation, MoviePlus for titles/transitions and Flash for artwork/SWF assets, among other tools. **This does not establish which software produced the supplied 2024 video.** The interview is also a vendor publication, so tool praise has promotional context. [Same 2018 interview](https://magazine.reallusion.com/2018/05/09/million-subscriber-youtuber-uses-crazytalk-for-sports-comedy-web-series/672375890/).

The creator's [official link page](https://linktr.ee/442oons) describes football cartoons, songs and parodies. A first-party [Footballco announcement](https://www.footballco.com/blog/footballcox442oons) includes Stobbart explaining that he created 442oons to celebrate football's funny side. These support a **football comedy** reference category, rather than an assumption that the work is an educational explainer or a realistic match simulation.

## Implications for the benchmark — interpretation

- Judge the combination of recognisable caricature, acted comedy and factual event anchors. Software choice alone cannot reproduce that combination.
- Establish the actual match or incident before writing each football film; record invented dialogue and visual gags separately from the match facts.
- Asset reuse is supported by creator testimony as a practical production method. It still needs new acting and staging for each joke.
- The reference's 116-second duration exceeds Pencil's present 32–60-second planning target. Its structure must be adapted to that shorter runtime or a longer film deliberately selected; do not silently inherit its duration.
- Samtoons is authorized as continuing similar animation work. Shared production identity does not by itself establish continuing plots or cliffhangers; each video can remain a complete standalone story under the workspace's [production terms](../../../CONTEXT.md).
