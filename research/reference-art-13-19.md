# Web artwork references for styles 13–19

Acquired and visually reviewed on 14 September 2026. These are attributed external quality references. A still establishes appearance, not motion performance; the existing Blender proofs remain separate. No footage was downloaded. Original artwork bytes are retained in the versioned reference gallery.

## midcentury

**Toot, Whistle, Plunk and Boom (1953) — musicians** — Walt Disney Productions; directed by Charles Nichols and Ward Kimball. [First-party source](https://d23.com/this-day/toot-whistle-plunk-and-boom-wins-oscar-for-best-short-subjectcartoon/) · [Original image](https://d23.com/app/uploads/2012/03/03.25.1954-toot-whistle-plunk-bloom-1180x600-1.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/midcentury-d23-oscar.jpg` (1180 × 600).

An unmistakable midcentury modernist animation scene: elongated angular musicians, delicate economical lines, flat pink/purple shapes and playful abstract instruments. D23 explicitly describes this Disney film as using the UPA style; it is not itself a UPA production.

Visible quality target:
- Playful exaggerated silhouettes built from long angular limbs and bold flat head and costume shapes.
- Economical fine contour and decorative lines over a restrained, harmonious pink-purple palette.
- Characters, instruments and scenery share stylized geometry and deliberate negative space.

The earlier Yuki 7 candidate is preserved at `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/midcentury-original.jpg`; it was replaced because its low-poly shapes were less immediately representative of classic midcentury design.

## whiteboard

**RSA Animate: Changing Education Paradigms** — Royal Society of Arts / Cognitive. [First-party source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zDZFcDGpL4U) · [Original image](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/zDZFcDGpL4U/maxresdefault.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/whiteboard-original.jpg` (1280 × 720).

The official RSA upload uses confident marker drawings, small expressive people, handwritten explanations and one accent color. Text is part of this visual treatment, not thumbnail decoration to reproduce.

Visible quality target:
- Confident dark contour lines on a clean white field, with readable small faces and hands.
- One restrained accent color that groups ideas and directs attention.
- Characters, diagrams and short labels arranged with clear spacing and an obvious reading order.

## storybook

**We’re Going on a Bear Hunt — ident artwork** — Lupus Films. [First-party source](https://www.lupusfilms.com/commercialsandidents/were-going-on-a-bear-hunt-ident) · [Original image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/642c2f96775b4a158c9f0456/1716306205055-LT4TQV4WVACPEO6N1AFL/Bear+Hunt+Cropped+without+text.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/storybook-original.jpg` (2500 × 1782).

This studio artwork puts expressive drawn people into a complete, textured landscape. It shows the intended finish at scene scale, including atmosphere and staging, rather than only a textured character.

Visible quality target:
- Visible pencil and watercolor-like grain that remains consistent across figures and scenery.
- Soft, harmonious color and value separation between the figures, foreground and distant landscape.
- Readable running poses and integrated ground contact inside a complete illustrated composition.

## collage

**Charlie and Lola — leaf costume and drawn character designs** — Lauren Child / Charlie and Lola production. [First-party source](https://milkmonitor.me/the-charlie-and-lola-television) · [Original image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5d9add0da2e4b826faa1d9ff/1578689095292-VWT1X2FRCD800B6R83G0/STORY_C%26L_TV_10.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/collage-original.jpg` (1500 × 1125).

A photographic leaf becomes a costume around an economical drawn face and limbs. This makes mixed-media collage unmistakable while preserving a coherent character and continuous limb connections.

Visible quality target:
- Photographic texture confined to a clear, deliberately cut silhouette.
- Hand-drawn faces, hair and limbs that retain one consistent visual scale.
- Clean overlaps and readable limb exits where the textured costume meets drawn body parts.

## pixelated

**Simpsons Pixels** — Ivan Dixon and Paul Robertson. [First-party source](https://www.ivandixon.com/) · [Original image](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/FIZ_gDOrzGk/maxresdefault.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/pixelated-original.jpg` (1280 × 720).

The creators’ pixel animation uses deliberate block shapes, a limited palette and clear silhouettes, with a fully designed living-room environment. This is pixel design, not simply a blur or mosaic filter.

Visible quality target:
- Consistent, crisp square pixel edges with no smeared or antialiased contours.
- Recognizable character silhouettes and faces using economical clusters of color.
- Environment, props and figures share the same pixel scale and palette discipline.

## motion-comic

**Sigma Origin Story — illustrated key image** — Blizzard Entertainment / Overwatch origin-story team. [First-party source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=onplsJSdp4A) · [Original image](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/onplsJSdp4A/maxresdefault.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/motion-comic-original.jpg` (1280 × 720).

A polished cinematic illustrated frame with a strong central figure, readable hands, controlled light and layered debris. It represents illustrated-film / motion-comic presentation, not proof that our rig reproduces its full anatomy or animation.

Visible quality target:
- One strong focal pose with readable face and hands against a controlled background.
- Deliberate shape-based shadows and highlights that support volume without muddying the silhouette.
- Foreground and background elements composed to create depth while keeping the story action clear.

## parallax

**Yuki 7 — Hong Kong style frame** — Elaine Lee / Chromosphere. [First-party source](https://chromosphere-la.com/case-study/yuki7/) · [Original image](https://chromosphere-la.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/chapter1_elaineHongKong-1920x1080.jpg).

Local asset: `/Users/samikd/MyProjects/Pencil/experiments/009_style_gallery/artifacts/reference-gallery-v001/downloads/parallax-original.jpg` (1920 × 1080).

A rich graphic scene with clear foreground vehicles, midground people and architecture, and receding streets. The still is a quality reference for layered staging; it cannot by itself demonstrate parallax, and the source production uses a more extensive 2D/3D hybrid pipeline.

Visible quality target:
- Clearly separated foreground, action plane and distant environment through scale, overlap and value.
- A controlled luminous palette that makes the intended focal area stand out.
- Environment detail and small figures designed in one coherent graphic style.

## Verification and scope

- Source pages were checked on the open web. Image content and resolution were inspected locally. YouTube stills were retrieved from the confirmed official/creator uploads; their 1280×720 files were checked after retrieval. The pixel-animation authorship is also stated on Ivan Dixon’s own site.
- Lauren Child’s own production explanation describes a paper world and shows design assets; Chromosphere credits the selected character designers and Hong Kong style-frame artist; Lupus Films identifies the Bear Hunt ident.
- Some initially considered Blender Studio artwork required subscription access. Some direct ArtStation and RSA page fetches returned 403. Those requests were not bypassed; accessible first-party alternatives or the official public upload images were used.
- Licensing has not been established for production reuse. Keep these images as references with credits.
