## Verification and further context

All main links returned their expected creator/studio titles and channel metadata through YouTube oEmbed. The 442oons link was checked separately in addition to the original 1–10 batch. Playback was not reviewed end to end. The rows identify visual principles worth studying; exact production methods, camera flexibility, redraws and deformation still need a focused proof in Pencil.

- GoogleDoodles' [video description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CkgirvbPJnw) identifies the silhouette and paper-cutout inspiration. The selection deliberately avoids relying on secondary historical summaries.
- Paramount's [announcement of the original South Park pilot](https://ir.paramount.com/news-releases/news-release-details/southparkstudioscom-unveils-web-only-exclusive-original-south) describes its construction-paper production. This supports the construction reference; it is not a claim that all later episodes use physical paper.
- The [official Pink Panther upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41aGCrXM20E) credits the character to Friz Freleng and David DePatie and identifies its MGM channel provenance.
- The [RSA upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zDZFcDGpL4U) credits Cognitive Media for the animation.
- Channel 4's [2022 ident announcement](https://www.channel4.com/press/news/snowmantm-melts-hearts-once-more-new-channel-4-christmas-ident) identifies this as a 20-second commissioned animation by Lupus Films and 4creative, celebrating the original film and its sequel.
- [CBeebies' Charlie and Lola page](https://www.cbeebies.com/shows/charlie-and-lola/) identifies the series. The linked YouTube clips return the official channel handle in oEmbed metadata.
- The [original Simpsons Pixels description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIZ_gDOrzGk) credits Robertson and Dixon and explicitly says that this was the original upload, later used by the television production. [Ivan Dixon's portfolio](https://www.ivandixon.com/) independently confirms those credits and the later licensing.
- Blizzard's [Sigma origin-story page](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/videos/video/blt0d4c9fb393f20b79/origin-story-sigma/) confirms the official short.
- Blender's [HERO upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pKmSdY56VtY) describes 2D animation tools within a full 3D pipeline. Animator [Sergi Miranda's project page](https://www.sergimiranda.com/hero/) describes his work on the film and its 2D animation in a 3D environment.
