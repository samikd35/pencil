# Working direction

- Use simple, expressive bodies. C / Elastic Comic is a proven animation reference, not a fixed visual identity. The user subsequently authorized recognizable player heads, hair, faces, kit details and changed proportions for Three Goals. One Heartbreak.
- Start from the current production's brief, selected references and existing work. Evaluate its actual shots and finished film through the shared workflow. Preserve `experiments/001_heavy_box/` and its delivery as historical evidence; its construction is an optional technical regression test when relevant, not a required starting point or style-quality benchmark.
- Keep character construction simple. Prioritize readable poses, continuous movement, contact, timing and expressive action. Each film's current user direction governs its visual style; preserve completed films and their assets.
- Keep football and educational topic exploration independent unless the user explicitly combines them.
- Football Shorts must retell verified real-world football incidents, famous moments or actual events. Preserve the important actions and outcome; distinguish visual exaggeration from historical fact. Choose the source event before developing its animated treatment.
- Educational animation may explain history, a mechanism or other factual information. It is not restricted to digital-life topics. Give each Short one clear question or learning outcome and verify the explanation.
- The earlier 32-60-second Shorts exploration is historical, not a limit on new productions. Samtoons is an explicitly selected football-comedy production; other niches remain available. Each production profile and current video brief determine audience, format and duration.
- Use the existing production journal for actual work. No speculative pipeline framework or database is needed.
- Treat `PyBlend_Stickman_Starter/` as historical reference only. Its creative choices, unfinished platform plans and approval policies do not govern new Pencil work. Apply skills and technique guides only when they fit the active film; a new niche need not use human characters or stick figures.

## Reusable production workflow

- For an authorized film or production revision, use `produce-pencil-animation`. Its authoritative source is [pipeline/SKILL.md](pipeline/SKILL.md); the installed skill links to that file. Follow it for joint picture/sound planning, creative checkpoints, scoped delegation and review. Edit the project source when improving the workflow.
- Use [productions/README.md](productions/README.md) for new normal production and video context; retain existing films and experiments in their current folders. A folder's name or contents never establishes series intent. Read [pipeline/README.md](pipeline/README.md) for execution conventions and [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) for the agreed production terms.
- Keep one general pipeline. Production profiles own niche/creative defaults; individual videos retain their adopted context, selected versions and explicit exceptions. Technique guides apply by actual medium/action across niches. Keep detailed domain rules in the relevant production rather than copying its creative defaults into the shared skill.
- When a construction defect cannot be corrected reliably without rebuilding, rebuild the affected construction and its dependent chain from scratch under [the shared conditional clean-rebuild rule](pipeline/SKILL.md#rebuild-when-construction-is-the-root-cause). Do not patch over or inherit the defective foundation; preserve historical files separately.
