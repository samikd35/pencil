## Landscape prompt

Output a landscape 16:9 image, ideally 1536x864. Image 1 is the exact Mbappe character/jersey identity reference. Image 2 is the exact Messi character, simplified body and trophy reference. Preserve this film's original illustrated character identity: polygonal but softened warm brown face, close cropped black hair, angular eyebrows, small almond eyes, simple dark ink limbs, navy France jersey with gold 10; Messi has brown swept hair and beard, simplified body, black and gold presentation robe. No realistic photo faces, no anime, no elaborate new character style.
Composition: Mbappe is the huge dominant foreground subject filling the left half and much of the height, head and upper torso only, visibly exhausted and crestfallen, lowered eyebrows at the outer ends, downturned mouth, gaze toward the trophy. Subtle sweat is fine, no invented crying. On the right, a much smaller celebrating Messi raises a large gleaming illustrated World Cup trophy. Ensure the trophy is held with anatomically coherent simple hands and Messi's face is visible below it. The strong visual contrast is three goals yet the other team gets the trophy. Use deep midnight navy/cool blue behind Mbappe and concentrated warm golden light around trophy/Messi; greatly simplify the stadium to atmospheric floodlight shapes, not detailed terraces. Bold clean silhouettes, ink outlines, refined 2D shading, dramatic rim light, very high contrast, restrained gold flecks only near the winner. Crisp readable at phone size.
Exact headline, no other text except jersey 10: "3 GOALS." then "STILL LOST." in extremely large heavyweight condensed sans serif. Put this two-line headline in the upper right without covering either face or trophy. First line white, second line vivid warm yellow, subtle dark stroke/shadow. Keep 6 percent safe margins. The trophy and winner occupy the lower right beneath the words. One clear composition, no small captions, scoreboards, arrows, watermarks, fake play buttons, channel logos, panels, excessive decorative elements or photorealism. Make it emotionally compelling while matching the animation people will actually watch.
