Heavy Box — color and sound finish

Open character-and-effects.blend in Blender 5.2 or later. Keep the audio folder beside it.

The project contains editable native character artwork, pose controls, illustrated lighting, soft shadows, golden sparks, and a stereo soundtrack strip starting at frame 1.

Audio is original procedural synthesis, not recorded speech. Separate footsteps, Foley, character-like tones, and electric-effect stems are included for remixing. soundtrack.wav is the normalized mix used by the project.

The exported movie is preview-with-sound.mp4. Frame range: 1–240 at 24 fps.
