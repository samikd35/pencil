## Craft to transfer into original scenes

| Area | Relevant observed example | What Samtoons must demonstrate |
|---|---|---|
| Visual storytelling and comedy | A recurring child treatment; B opening boast becomes humiliation | Clear expectation, trigger, changed information/status and payoff. Original gags with variation; no inherited episode ending. |
| Posing and expression | C ~0–4s lids/gaze; ~17s tense face; ~25s head-holding | The character appears to notice, think and react. Coordinate gaze, lids/brows, mouth, head and body; supply a needed drawing instead of keeping every role in its rest view. |
| Interaction | C ~5–11s seated exchange; B ~85–90s group reactions | Appropriate body construction for the situation, differentiated attitudes, eye-lines and listeners. Sitting is not a lowered standing puppet. |
| Physical movement | B ~14s, 21–26s, 56–57s, 75–76s; C ~30–33s sporting poses | Readable preparation, support/contact, release, follow-through and recovery. Confirm intermediate contacts with finer inspection; sampled poses alone do not prove mechanics. |
| Timing and editorial rhythm | B rapid impacts versus held reactions; deliberate slow-footage gag ~78s | Purposeful contrast in holds, snaps, pauses, overlap and cuts. Match the actual chosen voice and scene energy; no blanket slow easing. |
| Camera and composition | B ~64–68s pullback, ~69–71s reveal, ~80–83s close-ups; C television inserts | Clear focus and original coverage motivated by information. A cut, held frame and camera move serve different purposes. Preserve screen direction and prepare required views. |
| Character identity and clean construction | Coherent sampled silhouettes in B/C | Recognizable whole characters, expressive silhouettes, consistent proportions, clothes and shoes through transitions; no detached-looking heads, surface folds or generic replacement limbs. |
| Environment and depth | B pitch/net, stage and interview spaces | Economical location detail with coherent scale, perspective, layers, occlusion and ground contact. Sets support the action and remain consistent across cuts. |
| Art direction, color and light | The references' readable figures and simplified settings | A deliberate Samtoons treatment combining subjects, environment, line/shapes, value/color and shading. Review adjacent shots; added realism or light effects are not compulsory. |
| Secondary action and effects | B impact/prop reveals; C ~40s distinct celebration | Meaningful listener, eye, hand, clothing, crowd or effect choices that support focus. Avoid synchronized automatic motion and copied choreography. |
| Sound and presentation | Reference sound performance remains unverified | Audition new selected voices/effects/music in context. Preserve character identity and readable captions; actual listening and full encoded checks remain required by the common workflow. |
| Distinctive style and finish | Craft principles across A/B/C | Original Samtoons identity, polished transitions and purposeful detail. Borrow principles rather than exact characters, compositions, accents, music or jokes. |

At **staging**, show the unified visual treatment and a small representative sequence from the actual story. Cover ordinary acting/interaction, hard mechanics where present, the intended environment/look and meaningful coverage/edit continuity. Compare the whole result using the shared [quality matrix](../../pipeline/styles/construction.md#evaluate-the-current-production), not only a beauty frame or successful jump.

At **sound direction**, hear the actual candidates against the moment or explicitly retain unverified listening. At **animatic**, review the complete rough cut for performance variety, causality, escalation, rhythm and continuity. At **delivery**, review the exact encoded result, including sound, framing, details, transitions and captions. These remain the four existing decisions, not extra approvals.

Use normal-speed playback for intention/timing, consecutive frames for contact and transitions, and native-size crops for surface defects. State actual review coverage. For a fix, preserve likeness, coherent construction, acting, timing, contact and sound relationship together; a regression in another required quality keeps it unresolved. Compare new subjects/views/actions separately before propagation.
