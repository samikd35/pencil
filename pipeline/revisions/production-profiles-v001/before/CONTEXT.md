# Pencil production language

Terms distinguish creative intent from the organization of work.

## Language

**Production**: A named body of video work with applicable context and potentially reusable assets. It may contain a standalone film or an explicitly requested series; membership alone implies no story continuity.

**Video**: An individual authored work with its own brief, creative choices and review history. A film, Short or series episode can each be a video.

**Standalone**: A video developed as a complete work with its own premise, structure and ending. This is the default creative mode unless the user explicitly requests a series.

**Series**: Video work explicitly requested as a series, with the recurring format or continuity appropriate to that brief. Shared assets, numbering and multiple videos alone do not establish a series.

**Experiment**: Exploratory work that tests an idea, technique or production question. Its findings can inform later work without imposing its creative choices on that work.

**Pipeline**: The shared production method, maintained guidance and working mechanisms improved through research and actual production evidence.
