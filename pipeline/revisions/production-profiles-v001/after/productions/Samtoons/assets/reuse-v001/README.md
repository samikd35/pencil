## Character construction: extract from the delivered scene

The manifest pins episode 001's final editable `.blend`, picture reference, required end-card image and relevant repair scripts/checks without duplicating the large movie/scene. These are **scene-bound extraction references**, not portable character packages. The film's accepted animatic and final review history establish the visible performances within their actual scope; they do not approve every possible pose or turn.

For a new shot, identify the actual character collection, controls, artwork and materials in the pinned scene; inspect dependency paths and copy only required assets into that video's selected files. Preserve source/hash and the tested scope. Never execute these episode builders blindly: `runpy`/imports have film-specific state and dependencies. A genuinely portable rig requires extraction, save/reopen checks and the new shot's demanding-view/motion review before it can be labelled reusable without the original scene.

Use the [profile](../../README.md) and [general selection convention](../../../README.md#shared-assets-and-stable-selections). Keep accepted recordings cached when retiming. References and music from episode 001 are not silently adopted; select and verify the relevant provenance/licence for their actual new use.
